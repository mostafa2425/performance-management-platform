Introduction
The Performance Management Platform aims to provide a centralized solution for managing the employee performance lifecycle across the organization.
The platform will support the process from performance cycle setup and objective setting through Admin (Talent Development) review, employee review, performance evaluation, final approval, and publication of performance results.
It will enable Admin to manage and monitor the performance process, Managers to define and evaluate employee objectives, Employees to review their assigned objectives and performance results, and authorized management stakeholders to access performance information according to their organizational scope.
The platform will also support performance competencies, objective templates, performance rating rules, workflow history, notifications, and dashboards.

Objective of the project
The main objectives of the project are to:
Establish a centralized and standardized Performance Management Platform.
Enable Admin to configure, manage, and monitor performance cycles across the organization.
Enable Managers to create, assign, manage, and evaluate employee objectives.
Enable Admin to review and approve employee objectives before they are presented to Employees.
Enable Employees to review, approve, or request modifications to their assigned objectives.
Support structured performance evaluation based on Objectives and Competencies.
Provide visibility of performance progress and pending actions throughout the performance lifecycle.
Maintain a complete history and audit trail of approvals, returns, comments, and evaluation activities.
Improve transparency and consistency of the employee performance management process.
Ensure consistent and controlled performance rating distribution through Bell Curve monitoring and validation.

Purpose of the document
The purpose of this Business Requirements Document (BRD) is to document the business requirements for the Performance Management Platform and establish a common understanding of the required solution among business and project stakeholders.
The document defines the business users and roles, data requirements, business functions, end-to-end processes, business rules, workflow statuses, notifications, and related system dependencies.

Business Requirements Document Structure
The business requirements in this document are structured into key analysis areas to provide a clear and consistent view of the proposed Employee Performance Management Platform.
Each area addresses a specific aspect of the business requirements, from identifying the users and required data to defining the business functions, processes, rules, and notifications.

General Permission Rules
Rule
Description
Approved Objectives Lock
Once the Employee approves the Performance Plan, Objectives, KPIs, Targets, and Weights shall be locked from modification through the standard workflow.
As an exception, the Admin may return the approved Plan to the Manager for modification before the applicable Annual Evaluation stage begins. The Manager shall not be permitted to edit the approved Plan unless it has been explicitly reopened by the Admin.
Employees manually included in an Active Annual Performance Cycle after the standard Objective Setting stage has closed shall follow the standard Performance Plan creation and approval workflow without reopening the completed stage for other Employees.
Once the Employee approves the Performance Plan, Objectives, KPIs, Targets and Weights shall be locked from modification.
Completed Stage Lock
Admin cannot modify dates of completed stages. Only the current incomplete stage and future stage dates may be updated.
Admin History Access
Admin has full visibility of Workflow and Audit History.
Manager History Access
Manager may view Workflow History and Performance History for authorized Direct Reports.
Following a change in Direct Manager, the newly assigned Manager shall become responsible for the Employee's Annual Evaluation and shall have access to the Employee's authorized Performance Plan and relevant historical Performance information.
Employee History Access
Employee shall not have access to Workflow/Audit History “between manager and admin”.
Multiple Active Cycles
Admin may create multiple Active Performance Cycles. An Employee shall not be included in more than one Active Performance Cycle at the same time. A Manager may manage Employees across multiple Active Performance Cycles.
Configuration Permissions
Annual Performance Component Weights are configurable by Admin. Configuration changes shall not retroactively moodify finalized results.
Annual Final Review Return

When an Annual Evaluation Group is returned by Admin, the Manager may revise the applicable Annual Evaluation inputs for Employees within the returned Group. The system shall recalculate the affected Performance Scores, Ratings, and Bell Curve distribution. Resubmission shall be permitted only after the Group satisfies all required Bell Curve limits.When the Annual Performance Result is returned by Admin, the Manager may modify the evaluation of the final Period only. Objectives, KPIs, Targets, KPI Weights, and previously completed Period evaluations shall remain locked.
Annual Performance Result AccessFinalized Period Result Access

Annual Performance Results shall remain unpublished to Employees until the applicable Manager–Year Group has been approved by Admin and the results have been officially published.
Published Annual Performance Results shall be read-only and accessible according to the authorized role-based scope:
Employee: Own published Annual Performance Results only.
Manager: Published Annual Performance Results for authorized Direct Reports.
Department Manager: Published Annual Performance Results for Employees within the authorized Department scope.
Admin: Published Annual Performance Results for all Employees.
HCD: Published Annual Performance Results for Employees within the authorized organizational scope.
Upon completion and approval of each Performance Period, the Final Period Result shall be visible according to the applicable role-based scope: Employee for their own result, Manager for Direct Reports, Department Manager for Employees within the authorized Department scope, Admin and HCD for all Employees across the organization. Finalized Period Results shall be read-only.
Bell Curve Access

The Bell Curve distribution shall be visible to Managers from the start of the Annual Evaluation stage and updated whenever evaluation inputs change.
The system shall calculate the Minimum allowed Employee count by rounding down and the Maximum allowed Employee count by rounding up, based on the eligible Employee population assigned to the current Manager.
The Manager shall not submit the Annual Evaluation Group unless every Rating count satisfies both its Minimum and Maximum allowed limits.
The system shall not automatically calibrate Ratings or allow Managers to manually override calculated Ratings. Managers shall adjust the underlying KPI Actuals and/or Competency Ratings to achieve compliance.
These rules shall apply to all Managers regardless of team size, without exceptions.
Bell Curve calibration shall be applied to the applicable Manager's employee population using the defined Rating distribution limits.
Where a Rating exceeds its permitted allocation, the system shall compare Employees calculated Overall Annual Performance Scores, including decimal values, and move the lowest-scoring Employee(s) to the immediately lower Rating.
The process shall continue across lower Ratings as required to achieve the permitted distribution.
Bell Curve calibration shall not change the underlying calculated Overall Annual Performance Score. Bell Curve details shall not be visible to Employees.
Rating Scale Access

The Performance Rating Scale shall be visible to Managers as read-only reference information during applicable Performance Management activities.
Admin Performance Plan Editing
The Admin may create a Performance Plan for an Employee who has no existing Plan for the applicable Annual Performance Cycle, and may edit submitted Performance Plans during Admin Review. This permission shall be limited to Performance Plan data and shall not extend to KPI Actuals, Competency Ratings, calculated Performance Results, or other Evaluation Data.

Jisr Integration Business Rules
#
Business Rule
1
Jisr shall be the source of truth for Employee and organizational master data used by the Performance Management Platform.
2
Employee and organizational data shall be synchronized and kept updated in the Performance Management Platform.
3
Changes in Direct Manager, Job Title, Department, Business Unit, Location or Employment Status shall be reflected following successful synchronization.
4
Newly synchronized Employees shall become available in the platform's Employee records. Employees not included in an existing Annual Performance Cycle shall be identifiable and available for Admin review and manual Cycle inclusion, subject to the applicable exception rules.Newly added Employees shall become available for applicable Performance Cycle scope selection.
5
Inactive/terminated Employees shall not be deleted from historical Performance records.
Employees whose employment has ended shall be displayed with an Employment Status of "Terminated", based on the latest synchronized data from Jisr.
6
Historical Performance data shall remain preserved when Employee organizational information changes.
7
A newly synchronized Job Title with no Competency Template shall be identified and flagged to Admin. The applicable process shall not proceed until a Competency Template is assigned.
8
The system shall retain the latest synchronization status and identify failed synchronization records/reasons where applicable.
9
When selecting Employees for a Performance Cycle, the system shall use the latest successfully synchronized Employee information from Jisr.
10
Employee Eligibility and Exclusion
The system support identifying Employees who are not subject to Performance Evaluation based on available employee attributes synchronized from Jisr.
Exclusion categories include terminated Employees, Tamheer, GDP, and trainees, project-based staff, and Employees assigned to overseas offices.
Subject to the availability of reliable classification data from Jisr, Employees identified as excluded from Performance Evaluation should be excluded from the platform's active evaluation population and related employee counts and evaluation statistics.
The system retrieve the Employee's Employment Start Date from Jisr to calculate their length of service. If the Employee has completed less than six months of service, shall be flagged and excluded by default.
11
When an Employee's Direct Manager changes during an active Annual Performance Cycle, the Employee shall be reassigned to the new Manager for Annual Evaluation and Bell Curve purposes. The Employee's approved Performance Plan and historical records shall be preserved.
Succession Integration Business Rules
#
Business Rule
1
The Performance Management Platform shall retrieve the applicable Job Description based on the Employee's Job Title / Job Identifier.
2
Job Description shall be displayed as reference information and shall not be manually modified by the Manager within Performance Management.
3
If the Employee's Job Title changes through Jisr, the system shall retrieve the Job Description applicable to the updated Job Title.
4
Missing Job Description data shall not automatically block the Performance Cycle.

Authorized User
Description
This procedure enables authorized users to securely access the Performance Management Platform using their organizational account.
Pre-Condition
The user must have an active organizational account and be authorized to access the Performance Management Platform.
Business Constraints
Only authorized users can access the platform.
If the user accesses the platform through a notification link while not authenticated, authentication is required before accessing the requested page.
Activity Steps

The user accesses the Performance Management Platform.
The system displays the Login page.
The user selects one of the available login methods:
Enters organizational email and password and selects Login,
Selects Sign in with Microsoft.
The system authenticates the user using the selected authentication method.
Upon successful authentication, the system verifies that the user is authorized to access the platform.
The system identifies the user's assigned role(s) and permissions.
The user is granted access to the platform.
The system redirects the user to the appropriate page.

Notifications
No business notification is required. The system displays an appropriate message if authentication fails or the user is not authorized to access the platform.

Procedure Code
UC-002
User
Admin
Description

This procedure enables the admin to monitor the overall performance management process through the Dashboard, including employee performance status, active Performance Cycles, cycle progress, and Manager progress.

Pre-Condition

Admin has successfully logged in to the Performance Management Platform.
Employee and organizational data required for the Dashboard is available in the platform.

Business Constraints

The Dashboard shall provide an overall view across all applicable Performance Cycles.
Total Employees shall represent all employees.
Each employee shall be counted once under the applicable current performance status in the Employee Status Summary.
Employees who have not started the performance process shall be included under Not Started.
The admin shall be able to filter by status.
The admin shall be able to search.
Manager Progress shall reflect the employee records within the selected Dashboard filters.
Exported data shall reflect the applied search and filter criteria.
The Not Started Count shall identify Employees who are not currently included in any Active Performance Cycle.
The Admin shall be able to identify Employees excluded from the Annual Performance Cycle due to having less than six months of service, based on the Employment Start Date synchronized from Jisr.
Employees excluded from the Cycle shall not be included in the Cycle's active evaluation population or related evaluation progress counts unless manually included by the Admin.

Activity Steps
Admin accesses the Dashboard.
The system displays the Employee Status Summary.
The system displays the active Performance Cycles and their current progress.
The system displays the Manager Progress section.
Admin may filter by Status to filter the Manager Progress records.
Admin may search and filter the Manager Progress records.
The system refreshes the displayed records based on the applied criteria.
Admin may export the displayed Manager Progress data.
Admin may select a Performance Cycle to view its details.
Admin may select View Details for a Manager to view the Manager's employee records. Ref. UC-003

Notifications
No business notification is generated as part of this procedure.

Create Performance Cycle
Field
Details
Procedure Name
Create Performance Cycle
Procedure Code
UC-006
User
Admin
Description
This procedure enables the Admin to create an Annual Performance Cycle, define the applicable Employee scope, apply Employee eligibility and exclusion rules, configure the five workflow stage deadlines, and save the Cycle as Draft or activate it.
This procedure enables the Admin to create a Performance Cycle, define its frequency and period, select the employees included in the cycle, configure stage deadlines, review the generated periods, and save the cycle as Draft or activate it.
Pre-Condition
The Admin has access to create Performance Cycles.

Business Constraints

The Admin shall define the Cycle Start Date, and the system shall automatically calculate the Cycle End Date to complete one Performance Year.
The Cycle End Date shall be system-calculated and read-only.
Each Performance Cycle shall be annual and shall contain one Annual Performance Evaluation.
Cycle Frequency shall be Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual.
The Admin shall define the Cycle scope by selecting one or more Departments, Job Titles, and/or specific Employees.
Selecting a Job Title shall initially include the employees associated with that Job Title.
The Admin may exclude individual Employees from the selected Job Title(s)/department before activating the cycle.
The Admin may search for and add specific Employees individually to the cycle scope.
The final employee scope shall reflect the admin’s confirmed employee selection after additions and exclusions.
An Employee shall not appear more than once in the same Performance Cycle scope.
An Employee shall not be included in more than one Active Performance Cycle at the same time.
Employees who are already included in another Active Performance Cycle shall be clearly identified during scope selection and shall not be eligible for selection.
Performance Stages may overlap. The workflow shall proceed to the next applicable action once the previous action is completed, without waiting for the Stage Deadline. However, Manager Evaluation shall only start after Active Performance ends. All actions shall be closed once their applicable Stage Deadline is reached.
The Admin shall configure the five workflow stages once for the Annual Performance Cycle.The admin shall configure the Stage Deadlines for the first Performance Period only. The system shall automatically apply the same relative Stage Deadline pattern to all subsequent generated Performance Periods.

The system shall maintain one Annual Evaluation within each Annual Performance Cycle.Based on the selected Frequency, the system shall automatically generate the applicable Performance Periods within the Performance Cycle: four Periods for Quarterly, two Periods for Semi-Annual, or one Period for Annual.

The system shall automatically calculate the Start Date and End Date of each generated Performance Period within the Cycle.
The cycle cannot be activated unless all mandatory information and the final employee scope are valid.
Save as Draft shall save the cycle without activating it.
Upon successful activation, the Cycle Status shall change to Active.
During Cycle scope selection, the system shall retrieve the latest successfully synchronized Employee information from Jisr and apply the eligibility and exclusion rules defined in Section 9.2.
The system shall calculate each Employee's completed length of service as of the Cycle creation date.
Employees with less than six months of service shall be flagged and excluded from the proposed Cycle scope by default.
The Admin may manually include an Employee with less than six months of service in the proposed Annual Performance Cycle as an authorized exception before activation.
Employees excluded under other applicable exclusion categories shall remain excluded unless explicitly permitted by the approved eligibility rules.
The system shall distinguish Employees excluded due to insufficient length of service from Employees excluded under other applicable exclusion categories.
The Admin shall be able to review the proposed Employee scope and excluded Employees before activating the Cycle.
The final Employees and Managers in Scope shall be calculated based on the confirmed included Employees only.
Activity Steps
Admin selects Create Performance Cycle.
The system displays the Create Performance Cycle form.
Admin enters the Cycle Name and selects the Performance Year.
Admin selects the Frequency: Quarterly, Semi-Annual, or Annual.
Admin enters the Cycle Start Date, and the system automatically calculates the Cycle End Date to complete one Performance Year.
Admin defines the Cycle scope using one or more of the following options: Department, Job Title, and/or Specific Employees.
Department Scenario: Admin selects one or more Departments, and the system retrieves the Employees associated with the selected Department(s).
Job Title Scenario: Admin selects one or more Job Titles, and the system retrieves the Employees associated with the selected Job Title(s).
Specific Employee Scenario: Admin searches for and adds one or more Employees individually to the Cycle scope.
The system combines the selected scope and displays the number of Employees and Managers in Scope, without duplicating Employees included through more than one selection method.
The system identifies Employees who are already included in another Active Performance Cycle and displays the applicable Active Cycle Name. Such Employees shall not be eligible for selection in the new Performance Cycle.
Admin may select Preview Employees to review the Employees included in and excluded from the proposed Cycle scope, including their eligibility status and exclusion reasons.
Admin may select Preview Employees to review the Employees included in the Cycle scope.
Admin may exclude individual Employees from the generated scope or add additional specific Employees.
The system updates the final Employees and Managers in Scope based on the Admin's selections and exclusions.
Admin configures the required deadlines for the five workflow stages of the Annual Performance Cycle.
Admin configures the required Stage Deadlines for the first Performance Period.
The system validates the configured deadlines.
Based on the selected Frequency, the system generates and displays the applicable Performance Periods and automatically applies the same relative Stage Deadline pattern configured for the first Performance Period to all subsequent Periods.
Admin reviews the Annual Performance Cycle information, confirmed Employee scope, eligibility and exclusion details, and configured workflow stage deadlines
Admin reviews the Cycle information, final Employee scope, and generated Periods.
Admin selects either Save as Draft or Activate Cycle.
If Save as Draft is selected, the system saves the Cycle with status Draft.
If Activate Cycle is selected, the system validates the mandatory Cycle information, Employee scope, dates, and stage deadlines.
If validation is successful, the system activates the Performance Cycle and sets its status to Active.
The system displays a confirmation message.
Notifications
Upon successful activation, the system notifies the Managers that the Performance Cycle has been activated.
Upon successful activation, the system shall notify all Managers and Employees included in the Performance Cycle scope that the Performance Cycle has been activated.
